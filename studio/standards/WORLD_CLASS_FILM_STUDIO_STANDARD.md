# World-Class Film Studio — Internal Definition and Evaluation Standard
Revision 1.0 | 2026-11-03

## Meaning
A world-class studio is not an AI prompt with adjectives such as 'cinematic' or '8K'. It is a multidisciplinary creative organization that repeatedly translates vision into emotionally compelling, technically sound, coherent and deliverable work through leadership, collaboration and QA. There is no single globally certified 'world-class studio' label used as a pass/fail standard here.

## Twelve domains
1. Storytelling: audience engagement, causal dramaturgy, believable character stakes.
2. Direction: unified intent across departments.
3. Writing: clear narrative purpose, structure, dialogue, setup/payoff and revision.
4. Cinematography: deliberate framing, lens character, motivated light, blocking, movement and focus.
5. Production design: coherent world, costumes, hair, props, environment.
6. Performance: acting, emotion, authentic interactions, speaking/silent action.
7. Continuity: facial identity and wardrobe/prop/action/eyeline across shots.
8. Editorial: shot editability, coverage, dramatic rhythm, transitions and sequence flow.
9. Audio/music: dialogue clarity, sound field, room tone, emotional score and silence.
10. Production management: hierarchy, responsibilities, schedule, dependencies and handoff.
11. Media technical quality: calibrated specifications, metadata, backups, rights and QC.
12. Governance: approvals, revisions, decisions, accountability and critical review.

## Director leadership principle
User Executive Producer/Approver → AI Film Director (creative orchestration) ↔ 1st AD/Production Orchestrator (operational orchestration) → Specialist departments → Director Review → Human Gate.
A simulated specialist must not be represented as a staffed department.

## Quality doctrine
**Story First. Cinematic Craft Always. Continuity Everywhere. Quality at Every Gate.**
- Every scene must change something significant; every shot must fulfill a narrative/editorial function.
- Visual beauty alone cannot pass Story Quality.
- The director may reject contradictory or low-quality contributions and route revision to a named owner.
- Resolve user conflicts openly, e.g. 200 shots × 6–10 sec = 20–33m20s planned gross shot material before trimming; cannot promise 5 minutes without editing/change.
- User-provided identity refs are binding for all visible recurring characters; model limitations and uncertainty must be surfaced.
- Creative quality requires human evaluation; no numerical automated score certifies a world-class film.
