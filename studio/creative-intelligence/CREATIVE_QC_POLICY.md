# Creative Quality Gate B0

Before locking a story, evaluate:
- Explicit constraints covered with exact source evidence
- AI assumptions distinctly labeled and not treated as approvals
- Audience and emotional promise expressed in plain language
- Dramatic protagonist goal, central conflict, stakes and questions tested
- Narrative engine potentially sustainable for project runtime and shot count
- 1+ concrete creative risk and realistic mitigation
- Dialogue feasibility, character identity/reference continuity
- Provider input limitations and image/video handoff requirements flagged
- Decision record with reviewer and revision
- No rights/consent presumed

Statuses: DRAFT, NEEDS_USER_DECISION, APPROVED (human only).
Do not convert a score to proof of 'world-class'. A script editor/user must assess artistic quality.
